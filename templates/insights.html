Interpretation

The closer points stay to the diagonal reference line, the more closely the model follows the held-out observations. The final Ridge Regression model achieves an R² of 0.9375 on these 87 unseen vehicles, with an average error of 19.19 km.

Scatter is relatively uniform across the 135–685 km range, suggesting the model generalizes well to both compact and large-battery EVs.

04

Engineering Interpretation

Battery

Higher battery capacity provides strong predictive signal for increased range, consistent with the fundamental physics of stored energy available for propulsion.

Vehicle Dimensions

Height contributes some predictive signal, plausibly reflecting the vehicle's physical and aerodynamic characteristics. Larger frontal area may correlate with specific vehicle categories.

Performance

Torque and fast-charging characteristics contribute additional signal to the model's predictions, likely capturing drivetrain efficiency and battery thermal management capacity.

Feature importance indicates model reliance during training, not physical causation. Correlated features can share importance; permutation importance does not establish causality or held-out predictive contribution on its own.
