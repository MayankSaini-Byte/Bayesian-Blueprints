Pipeline Architecture

Raw specifications are cleaned, transformed, and passed through one reproducible preprocessing + modeling pipeline.

1Raw SpecificationsBattery, dimensions, performance, configuration
2Feature Engineeringfootprint_m2, volume_m3, battery_per_seat, torque_per_100kwh, segment_group
3Column TransformerNumeric: median impute → StandardScaler
Categorical: constant “Unknown” → one-hot
4Ridge Regressionα = 1
5Predicted RangeEstimated km

05

Feature Engineering

Five domain-specific features derived from raw specifications.

footprint_m2

(length_mm / 1000) × (width_mm / 1000)

Vehicle ground footprint area — proxy for aerodynamic drag area.

volume_m3

footprint_m2 × (height_mm / 1000)

Approximate vehicle spatial envelope.

battery_per_seat

battery_capacity_kWh / seats

Energy capacity allocated per passenger.

torque_per_100kwh

(torque_nm / battery_kWh) × 100

Drivetrain performance versus efficiency tuning ratio.

segment_group

prefix of segment

Broad market classification reducing cardinality.

07

Engineering Notes

Rated vs real-world range — The target represents official rated range. Real conditions (temperature, HVAC, terrain, driving) cause variation.
Missing cell metadata — number_of_cells is missing in 42.3% of vehicles; handled via imputation + missingness flag.
Cargo normalization — Three cargo entries recorded in banana boxes were treated as missing (NaN) rather than converted, to avoid fabricating data.
